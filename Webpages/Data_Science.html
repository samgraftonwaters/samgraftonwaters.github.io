Data Science

After completing the ONS Data Science Graduate Programme, I decided that this was the career for me. I was excited to learned about machine learning modelling, AI, NLP processes and much more. I found that as an analyst, I could explore the data as of now, but with Data Science I could use the data today to determine what could happen in the future. As a physicist, and knowing time travel is not possible, this is the closest thing to it - how fun!

During my role at CQC, I have applied what I have learned on the ONS Data Science Graduate Course in to projects in order to help with regulator activities. My first project explored serious injury notifications, using NLP and vectorisation processes to use classification models to class each notification as a certain injury. My next project used Large Language Models to classify LFPSE incidents, within Microsoft AI Foundry. Both these projects allowed me to explore both traditional machine learning models, and use newer AI tools.
